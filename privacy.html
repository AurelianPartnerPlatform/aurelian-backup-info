Privacy Policy

Aurelian Brain Backup is a private backup utility operated by its owner.

Google Drive Access

The application uses Google OAuth and rclone to access Google Drive for owner-authorized backup and recovery operations.

Data Storage

Backup files are stored locally and in the owner's authorized Google Drive account. This public website does not store backup files.

Data Sharing

The application is not offered as a public service. Backup data is not intentionally shared with advertisers or sold to third parties.

Access and Control

The owner controls the Google account, backup files, and OAuth permissions. Access can be revoked through Google Account security settings.
